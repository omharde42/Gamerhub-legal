## 1. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY OWNERSHIP FRAMEWORK

All intellectual property (IP), proprietary assets, digital creations, source code, designs, brand marks, and administrative accounts created for, incorporated into, or associated with **GamerHub** are the exclusive property of GamerHub (`[LEGAL ENTITY NAME]`).

No individual participant—including founders, co-founders, marketing personnel, website managers, contractors, or contributors—shall claim personal ownership over GamerHub IP by virtue of having authored, created, designed, hosted, or managed such assets.

---

## 2. SCOPE OF IP ASSETS

### 2.1 Source Code & Technical Infrastructure
GamerHub IP includes all software and technical artifacts created for GamerHub, including:
* **Frontend Code:** React, Vue, HTML5, CSS3, JavaScript, TypeScript, mobile apps, and client-side scripts.
* **Backend Code:** Node.js, Python, Go, Java, PHP, microservices, serverless functions, and backend APIs.
* **APIs & Integrations:** Custom APIs, webhooks, third-party API integration wrappers, and SDKs.
* **Scripts & Utilities:** Build scripts, database migration scripts, automated tools, and deployment scripts.
* **Database Structures:** Schemas, ER diagrams, SQL scripts, NoSQL collections, indexes, and stored procedures.
* **Documentation:** Technical architecture diagrams, API documentation, developer guides, and system manuals.
* **Infrastructure as Code:** Configuration files, Dockerfiles, Kubernetes manifests, CI/CD pipelines, and Terraform configurations.

### 2.2 UI/UX, Designs & Creative Assets
GamerHub IP includes all design work created for GamerHub, including:
* **UI/UX Assets:** Wireframes, interactive prototypes, user journey maps, design tokens, and style guides.
* **Graphics & Artwork:** Logos, vector icons, banners, UI badges, avatars, background illustrations, and game-related artwork.
* **Marketing Materials:** Promotional banners, ad creatives, video trailers, social media overlays, and email templates.

### 2.3 Brand & Identity Assets
GamerHub IP includes all branding elements, registered or unregistered:
* The name **"GamerHub"** and associated stylized variations.
* Primary and secondary GamerHub logos, logomarks, and favicons.
* Project taglines, slogans, mission statements, and brand guidelines.
* Social media account handles (`@GamerHub`, etc.) across all current and future platforms.

### 2.4 Domain Names
* Primary domain names (including `gamerhub.[tld]` and all associated subdomains, TLD extensions, and defensive registrations) are exclusive GamerHub IP.
* Domain names must be registered under GamerHub's official legal name/entity or registered jointly under Founder and Co-Founder administrative ownership on behalf of GamerHub.
* Domain control shall never be held under a private individual account without shared administrative back-up credentials.

### 2.5 Administrative Accounts & Digital Infrastructure
Ownership and ultimate control of all operational platforms belong exclusively to GamerHub:
* **GitHub / Source Control:** Primary GitHub Organization, private/public repositories, Organization Owner accounts.
* **Cloud & Hosting Services:** AWS, Google Cloud, Vercel, Netlify, Render, DigitalOcean hosting accounts.
* **Database Platforms:** Managed DB accounts (MongoDB Atlas, Supabase, Firebase, AWS RDS).
* **Social Media Accounts:** Twitter/X, Discord servers, YouTube, Instagram, Twitch, LinkedIn, TikTok, Facebook.
* **Email & Analytics:** Google Workspace/domain email accounts, Google Analytics, PostHog, Mixpanel accounts.
* **Payment & Financial Accounts:** Stripe, PayPal, Razorpay, bank accounts.

## 3. ABSOLUTE SAFEGUARD AGAINST ACCOUNT LOCKOUT

**Crucial Policy:** No individual team member—regardless of role—shall be able to permanently take control of, lock out, or withhold access to GamerHub’s critical digital assets (source code, domains, social media accounts, hosting platforms) merely because they created, registered, or currently manage the account.

All master administrative accounts must use official GamerHub credentials and require dual administrative recovery access shared between the Founder (Yash) and Co-Founder (Om Harde).

---

## 4. WORK PRODUCT VS. PRE-EXISTING WORK

### 4.1 GamerHub Work Product
Any code, design, text, artwork, graphic, database structure, or marketing asset created specifically for GamerHub by any party during their involvement is deemed **"GamerHub Work Product"** and is automatically assigned to GamerHub in full upon creation.

### 4.2 Pre-Existing Work
* If a team member incorporates pre-existing open-source libraries, personal code modules, or third-party assets created prior to joining GamerHub into GamerHub ("Pre-Existing Work"), the contributor retains original ownership of that pre-existing work.
* However, by incorporating Pre-Existing Work into GamerHub, the contributor automatically grants GamerHub a perpetual, worldwide, non-exclusive, royalty-free, transferable, sublicensable license to use, modify, compile, display, and distribute said Pre-Existing Work as part of GamerHub.

---

## 5. REVENUE AND MANAGEMENT SEPARATION FROM IP OWNERSHIP

* **Operational Management:** Managing a website, managing social media channels, or hosting servers does **not** grant the manager any intellectual property ownership in those assets.
* **Title Separation:** Operational titles (such as "Website Manager" or "Lead Marketer") convey functional responsibilities, not ownership title to GamerHub IP.
