## 1. PURPOSE & APPLICABILITY

This Confidentiality and Non-Disclosure Agreement ("NDA") protects all proprietary, non-public, technical, operational, and commercial information of **GamerHub** (`[LEGAL ENTITY NAME]`).

This Agreement binds all project participants, including Founder **Yash**, Co-Founder **Om Harde**, Marketing & Website Management Lead **Purvesh Bhadale**, as well as any future employees, contractors, advisors, and open-source contributors.

---

## 2. DEFINITION OF CONFIDENTIAL INFORMATION

"Confidential Information" includes all non-public information disclosed by or relating to GamerHub, whether oral, written, electronic, or visual, including without limitation:

### 2.1 Source Code & Technical Infrastructure
* Private GitHub repositories, branches, commit logs, pull requests, and unreleased source code (frontend, backend, APIs, scripts).
* Database structures, schemas, connection strings, data models, and migration scripts.
* System architecture documents, network topology, server configurations, Dockerfiles, and CI/CD pipelines.
* Unreleased features, internal algorithms, game engine integrations, and technical research.

### 2.2 Credentials & Security Access Keys
* API keys, OAuth client secrets, access tokens, JWT secrets, and database passwords.
* Server SSH keys, SSL certificates, cloud platform root/IAM credentials.
* Domain registrar passwords, hosting control panel logins, CMS admin logins, and social media login credentials.
* Security incident logs, vulnerability reports, and audit results.

### 2.3 Business, Financial & Strategic Information
* Product roadmaps, unreleased platform updates, and feature release dates.
* Financial metrics, revenue figures, profit sharing terms, bank details, and payment processor accounts.
* Investor decks, business plans, monetization strategies, and pricing models.
* Internal meeting minutes, decision logs, and legal dispute discussions.

### 2.4 User & Customer Data
* User profile information, personal identifiable information (PII), email addresses, hashed passwords, IP logs, payment transaction records, and user analytics.

### 2.5 Legal & Partnership Agreements
* Terms of unreleased commercial deals, sponsorship agreements, influencer contracts, and partner proposals.

---

## 3. OBLIGATIONS & RESTRICTIONS

All parties subject to this Agreement agree to:
1. **Strict Confidentiality:** Maintain Confidential Information in strict confidence using a reasonable standard of care (at least equal to that used for their own sensitive data).
2. **Limited Purpose:** Use Confidential Information exclusively to perform authorized GamerHub responsibilities.
3. **No Unauthorized Disclosure:** Refrain from disclosing, publishing, sharing, leaking, or transferring Confidential Information to any unauthorized third party without prior written consent from the Founder or Co-Founder.
4. **No Reverse Engineering or Unauthorized Copying:** Refrain from copying, scraping, storing, or exporting private repositories or databases onto unapproved personal hardware or public platforms.
5. **Prompt Incident Reporting:** Immediately notify the Founder (Yash) and Co-Founder (Om Harde) upon discovering any unauthorized disclosure, credential leak, data breach, or security vulnerability.

---

## 4. EXCLUSIONS FROM CONFIDENTIAL INFORMATION

Confidential Information does not include information that:
1. Is or becomes publicly available through no breach of this Agreement by the receiving party.
2. Was already in the receiving party's lawful possession prior to disclosure by GamerHub without confidentiality restrictions.
3. Is independently developed by the receiving party without reliance on or reference to GamerHub Confidential Information.
4. Is required to be disclosed by applicable law or valid legal court order, provided the receiving party gives prompt written notice to GamerHub to seek a protective order.

---

## 5. DURATION & SURVIVAL OF OBLIGATIONS

* The confidentiality obligations under this Agreement shall take effect immediately upon exposure to Confidential Information and shall remain in effect during the party's involvement with GamerHub.
* **Post-Termination Survival:** Upon departure, exit, or removal from GamerHub, the obligation to protect trade secrets, source code, user data, and security credentials shall **survive indefinitely** or for the maximum period permitted under applicable law (`[GOVERNING LAW / JURISDICTION]`).

---

## 6. RETURN OR DESTRUCTION OF CONFIDENTIAL MATERIALS

Upon departure or written request from GamerHub leadership, the departing party must:
1. Immediately surrender all physical and digital documents containing Confidential Information.
2. Delete all copies of source code, credentials, customer data, and internal files from personal devices and cloud accounts.
3. Provide written certification confirming complete return or deletion of all GamerHub Confidential Information within 5 calendar days.
