## 2. PURPOSE OF THE AGREEMENT

This Agreement is established to achieve the following core objectives:

1. **Define Roles:** Clearly articulate the operational duties, boundaries, and leadership structures of all parties.
2. **Establish IP Rules:** Establish clear ownership rules for intellectual property, source code, designs, branding, and platform assets.
3. **Define Responsibilities:** Provide unambiguous functional expectations for development, management, and marketing.
4. **Protect Assets:** Safeguard GamerHub’s source code, databases, cloud infrastructure, and core digital assets against unauthorized transfer or loss.
5. **Domain & Website Rules:** Define operational workflows and ownership boundaries for domain names and website access.
6. **Confidentiality:** Enforce strict confidentiality protocols for source code, API keys, credentials, user data, and business strategies.
7. **Financial Framework:** Define revenue handling, expense allocation, budgeting approvals, and reimbursement workflows.
8. **Decision Procedures:** Establish clear governance procedures for routine operational decisions versus major strategic decisions.
9. **Exit Procedures:** Provide clear protocols for voluntary departure, inactivity, or removal of any team member.
10. **Prevent Disputes:** Avoid misunderstandings through explicit, written governance standards and dispute-resolution workflows.

*Note: All percentages and voting rights must be explicitly agreed upon and recorded in writing. Title alone does not convey equity.*

### 3.2 Legal Classification of Roles & Status

To prevent legal ambiguity, the parties explicitly recognize the distinction between operational roles and legal/financial rights:

* **Founder Status:** Refers to initial project creation, strategic direction, and executive leadership. Founder status alone does not grant unrestricted legal authority to alter agreed equity allocations unilaterally.
* **Co-Founder Status:** Refers to joint foundational contribution and executive technical ownership. Co-Founder status confers specific strategic consent rights as outlined herein.
* **Employee / Contributor / Contractor Status:** Service provision or operational execution (such as marketing management or website upkeep) does not constitute an equity grant unless formally assigned via written agreement.
* **Ownership / Equity:** Represents formal percentage legal title to GamerHub’s assets, profits, and enterprise value. Equity vesting, if applicable, shall be governed by a separate Vesting Schedule document.
* **Voting Rights:** Defines governance authority on major project decisions, distinct from percentage of financial distributions.
* **Management Authority:** Refers strictly to day-to-day operational execution permissions within assigned scopes.

## 6. LIMITATION OF AUTHORITY

No party may independently represent, obligate, or bind GamerHub in major commercial, financial, or legal commitments beyond their explicit authorized responsibilities. Specifically, no individual party may take any of the following actions without formal joint approval:
1. Executing binding commercial agreements or legal contracts.
2. Accepting third-party investment or issuing equity/debt.
3. Transferring, licensing, or encumbering GamerHub intellectual property.
4. Selling, pledging, or transferring GamerHub digital assets, domains, or repositories.
5. Incurring financial liabilities or loans in the name of GamerHub.

## 7. DECISION-MAKING FRAMEWORK

Operational and strategic decisions are classified into two distinct tiers:

### 7.1 Routine Decisions (Independent Operational Authority)
Routine operational decisions within assigned functional scopes may be handled independently:
* **Founder (Yash):** Overall strategic adjustments, brand messaging, operational prioritization.
* **Co-Founder (Om Harde):** Technical stack selection, code refactoring, infrastructure optimizations, pull request approvals, bug fixes.
* **Marketing & Website (Purvesh Bhadale):** Social media post scheduling, basic website text updates, routine community moderation, campaign content creation.

### 7.2 Major Decisions (Mandatory Consensus / Consent Required)
Major decisions require formal written consensus between the Founder (Yash) and Co-Founder (Om Harde), and where applicable, approval of the equity holders according to agreed voting thresholds (`[VOTING PERCENTAGE REQUIRED]`):
1. **Sale or Transfer:** Selling, merging, or transferring GamerHub or any of its core assets.
2. **Equity & Ownership Changes:** Issuing new equity, admitting new co-founders/partners, or altering existing ownership percentages.
3. **External Investment:** Accepting capital investment, venture funding, or loans.
4. **Domain & Core Asset Transfers:** Transferring ownership or control of primary domain names (`gamerhub.[tld]`), primary GitHub organization, or cloud hosting accounts.
5. **Platform Closure:** Liquidation, dissolution, or permanent shutdown of GamerHub.
6. **Intellectual Property Licensing:** Granting exclusive licenses to third parties for GamerHub code, graphics, or trademarks.
7. **Major Commercial Contracts:** Entering into commercial deals or vendor contracts exceeding `[MONETARY THRESHOLD]`.

## 9. LEAVING GAMERHUB (EXIT & DEPARTURE PROCEDURES)

### 9.1 Departure Triggers
A party’s active involvement in GamerHub may terminate upon:
1. Voluntary resignation in writing.
2. Uncured inactive status / failure to perform agreed duties for a period exceeding `[NUMBER]` consecutive days.
3. Removal for cause or without cause under Clause 10.

### 9.2 Obligations Upon Departure
Upon departure or termination of operational role, the departing party must:
1. **Credentials:** Immediately surrender all administrative access passwords, tokens, keys, and 2FA keys.
2. **Access Revocation:** Permit immediate removal of access from GitHub, servers, hosting, analytics, domains, and social media accounts.
3. **Asset Handover:** Transfer all GamerHub code, designs, credentials, graphics, customer lists, and documents in their possession.
4. **Data Deletion:** Delete all copies of GamerHub source code, databases, and confidential data from personal devices, certifying deletion in writing.
5. **Ongoing Confidentiality:** Maintain strict confidentiality regarding GamerHub trade secrets, source code, and internal documents indefinitely.
6. **Financial Settlement:** Receive payment for any verified, pre-approved outstanding expenses or earned distributions due up to the effective date of departure (`[OUTSTANDING PAYMENTS TERMS]`).

### 9.3 Restrictive Covenants / Non-Compete Note
*Any proposed non-compete clause or post-departure operational restriction MUST be explicitly reviewed by legal counsel for enforceability under applicable local labor and business law (`[GOVERNING LAW / JURISDICTION]`).*

## 10. REMOVAL FROM OPERATIONAL ROLE

### 10.1 Removal Process
A team member may be removed from their operational role upon:
1. Documented failure to perform core responsibilities or material breach of this Agreement, NDA, or Security Policies.
2. Formal written notification detailing the reason for removal.
3. Approval by the required consensus (`[REMOVAL CONSENT REQUIREMENT]`).

### 10.2 Transition Protocol
Upon notice of removal:
* Immediate credential rotation and access restriction shall be conducted by the Co-Founder/Founder.
* A mandatory 5-business-day handover window shall take place for project continuity.

### 10.3 Separation of Operational Role Removal from Equity Rights
Removal from an operational role (e.g., termination of website handling duties) does **not** automatically strip an individual of vested legal equity ownership rights, if any exist. Unvested equity or buyback options shall be governed strictly by the formal Vesting / Equity Buyback Agreement (`[EQUITY BUYBACK TERMS]`).

## 11. DISPUTE RESOLUTION PROCEDURE

In the event of any disagreement, claim, or dispute arising out of or relating to this Agreement, the parties shall strictly adhere to the following multi-stage resolution process:

1. **Stage 1 — Informal Internal Discussion:** The aggrieved parties shall meet in good faith to resolve the issue informally within 14 calendar days of written notice of dispute.
2. **Stage 2 — Written Statement of Dispute:** If unresolved, the disputing party shall submit a formal written document detailing the grievances, desired remedies, and relevant contractual provisions.
3. **Stage 3 — Good-Faith Executive Negotiation:** Founder (Yash) and Co-Founder (Om Harde) shall hold formal negotiations to reach an equitable settlement within 30 calendar days.
4. **Stage 4 — Independent Mediation:** If negotiation fails, the parties agree to submit the dispute to a neutral, independent mediator selected by mutual agreement in `[GOVERNING LAW / JURISDICTION]`. Mediation costs shall be split equally among the parties.
5. **Stage 5 — Binding Arbitration / Legal Proceedings:** If mediation does not resolve the dispute within 60 days of commencement, any party may initiate formal legal proceedings in the competent courts of `[GOVERNING LAW / JURISDICTION]`.
