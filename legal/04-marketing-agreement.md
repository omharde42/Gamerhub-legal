## 1. PARTIES & SCOPE

This Marketing & Public Relations Agreement defines the responsibilities, governance limits, and approval workflows for **Purvesh Bhadale** in his role as **Marketing Lead** for GamerHub, under the executive direction of **Yash** (Founder) and technical alignment of **Om Harde** (Co-Founder).

---

## 2. DUTIES & RESPONSIBILITIES

Purvesh Bhadale shall manage GamerHub’s marketing, audience growth, and community outreach, including:
* **Social Media Management:** Creating, scheduling, and publishing posts across GamerHub social media accounts (Twitter/X, Discord, Instagram, Twitch, YouTube, TikTok, LinkedIn).
* **Content Creation:** Authoring promotional announcements, community newsletters, blog posts, gaming event graphics, and marketing copy.
* **Campaign Strategy:** Designing and executing marketing campaigns to expand GamerHub's user base and community engagement.
* **Community Growth:** Moderating official Discord servers, managing community events, responding to user inquiries, and promoting positive community guidelines.
* **Partnership Outreach:** Identifying prospective gaming influencers, esports teams, brand partners, and community sponsors.
* **Marketing Analytics:** Tracking key performance indicators (KPIs), campaign engagement metrics, conversion rates, and presenting periodic growth reports to the Founder and Co-Founder.

---

## 3. MANDATORY APPROVAL REQUIREMENTS

### 3.1 Commercial Partnerships & Sponsorships
Purvesh Bhadale must obtain written approval from the Founder (Yash) prior to entering into, signing, or announcing any commercial partnership, brand sponsorship, esports tournament affiliation, or advertising agreement.

### 3.2 Paid Advertising & Budget Expenditures
* No paid advertising campaigns (e.g., Meta Ads, Google Ads, Twitter Ads, Influencer payouts) may be launched without prior written budget approval from the Founder (Yash).
* All promotional expenses must adhere to the reimbursement protocols outlined in `legal/01-founder-cofounder-agreement.md`.

### 3.3 Public Relations & Brand Statements
Official press releases, major crisis management statements, legal notifications, or public announcements altering GamerHub policies must be reviewed and approved by the Founder prior to publication.

---

## 4. SOCIAL MEDIA & BRAND ASSET OWNERSHIP

* **Account Ownership:** All social media channels, handles (`@GamerHub`), Discord servers, mailing lists, and marketing accounts created for GamerHub belong exclusively to GamerHub (`[LEGAL ENTITY NAME]`).
* **Credential Sharing:** Admin credentials and recovery emails/phone numbers for primary marketing channels must be registered under official GamerHub accounts and shared with the Founder (Yash).
* **Non-Ownership:** Execution of marketing duties or rapid growth of social channels does **not** grant the Marketer personal legal ownership over GamerHub handles, content, or brand assets.
