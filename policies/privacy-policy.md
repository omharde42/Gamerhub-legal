## 1. INTRODUCTION & SCOPE

## 2. INFORMATION WE COLLECT

We adhere to the principle of **data minimization**—collecting only the personal information necessary to deliver and secure our services.

### 2.1 Information You Directly Provide
* **Account Credentials:** Username, email address, password hashes, and profile preferences upon account creation.
* **Profile & Gaming Information:** Gamertags, linked gaming handles, bio, avatar graphics, and user community posts.
* **Support Communications:** Messages, bug reports, and correspondence sent to GamerHub support.

### 2.2 Information Collected Automatically
* **Log & Technical Data:** IP address, browser type, operating system, device identifiers, referring URLs, and access timestamps.
* **Usage Data:** Pages viewed, platform navigation paths, game interactions, feature usage, and session durations.

### 2.3 Payment Information
* Purchases, subscriptions, or donations are processed via authorized third-party payment gateways (e.g., Stripe, PayPal).
* GamerHub does **not** collect or store full credit card numbers or sensitive financial verification codes on our servers.

## 3. HOW WE USE YOUR INFORMATION

We process collected data exclusively for legitimate operational purposes:
1. To create, maintain, and authenticate user accounts.
2. To provide core gaming features, leaderboards, and community interaction tools.
3. To process legitimate financial transactions and send purchase receipts.
4. To monitor platform health, mitigate security threats, prevent fraud, and enforce our Terms of Service.
5. To send essential service notifications, updates, and transactional communications.
6. To analyze aggregate platform trends and improve user interface performance.
