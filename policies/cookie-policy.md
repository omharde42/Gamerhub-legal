## 1. WHAT ARE COOKIES AND LOCAL STORAGE?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit websites. GamerHub uses cookies and modern browser storage technologies (such as `localStorage` and `sessionStorage`) to enable essential website functions, remember user preferences, maintain active login sessions, and analyze site traffic.

---

## 2. CATEGORIES OF COOKIES WE USE

GamerHub categorizes cookies and storage technologies into the following types:

### 2.1 Strictly Necessary / Essential Cookies
* **Purpose:** Essential for the platform to function properly. Includes session authentication tokens, security anti-CSRF tokens, and load balancer routing.
* **Opt-Out:** These cannot be disabled because core site navigation and account login depend on them.

### 2.2 Performance & Analytics Cookies
* **Purpose:** Help us measure site traffic, page load performance, error rates, and user navigation trends to improve GamerHub performance.
* **Opt-Out:** Users may opt out via browser settings or analytics preference toggles.

### 2.3 Preference & Functional Cookies
* **Purpose:** Remember user choices such as dark/light mode display settings, language preferences, and audio volume levels.

## 3. THIRD-PARTY COOKIES & EMBEDDED CONTENT

GamerHub may integrate third-party services that set cookies independently:
* **Analytics Providers:** Google Analytics, PostHog.
* **Payment Gateways:** Stripe, PayPal checkout widgets.
* **Media & Streaming:** Embedded Twitch streams, YouTube trailers, Discord widgets.

Third-party cookie practices are governed strictly by the respective privacy policies of those third-party providers.

---

## 4. MANAGING YOUR COOKIE PREFERENCES

You can manage or disable non-essential cookies through:
1. **Browser Settings:** Adjusting your web browser preferences to decline or delete cookies.
2. **Preference Banners:** Utilizing GamerHub’s cookie preference banner where applicable.

*Note:* Disabling strictly necessary cookies may prevent you from logging in or using GamerHub features properly.
